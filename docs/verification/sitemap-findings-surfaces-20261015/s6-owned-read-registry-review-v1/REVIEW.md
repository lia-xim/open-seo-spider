## Bounded no-additional-findings result

- Admission, close and retained supervisor handles share one mutex. The same actual Core control is cloned into blocking operation, supervisor, retained Task and immediate CancelOnDrop. No await separates admission from arming the request Drop guard, and no mutex guard is held over an await.
- The async supervisor owns the real spawn_blocking JoinHandle. Request closure or timer branch calls stop and then awaits that handle. No success/error result is delivered before worker join. After join the Joined guard covers classification/result destruction panic, decrements active and notifies drains. A real worker panic is a joined JoinError. Unexpected completed supervisor errors are retained and surfaced by drain.
- Concurrent drains close admission and cancel controls without removing unfinished handles. Cancelling a drain future drops no retained supervisor handle. Reaping removes only is_finished handles and polls them to resolution. Current lock-bound Tokio1.53.2 JoinHandle::is_finished reads actual completion state. No completed-output or cancellation timing was executed.
- Notify registration/enable occurs before completion predicates. Active0 with an unfinished supervisor handle yields rather than awaiting a final notification that may already have happened. Multiple drains register separately, and handles stay in shared state. Poison recovery retains ownership and records the first failure. These are static paths, not observed scheduler/race proof.

They cover worker Drop-before-result, request cancellation with held worker, async deadline while worker remains held, concurrent/cancelled drain, worker panic, typed operation errors, reaping, and past Notify/concurrent drains.
